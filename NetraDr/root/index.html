The Problem

Why NetraDR Exists

India has the second-largest diabetic population in the world. Diabetic Retinopathy is the leading cause of preventable blindness — yet most cases go undetected until vision loss is irreversible.

The Crisis

Over 77 million Indians have diabetes. Nearly 1 in 3 will develop some form of Diabetic Retinopathy. Most don't know until it's too late.

The Gap

India has approximately 1 ophthalmologist per 100,000 people. In rural areas, the ratio is even worse. Screening infrastructure barely exists outside cities.

The Solution

NetraDR uses AI to bring retinal screening to primary health centres, screening camps, and remote locations — making early detection accessible and affordable.

Workflow

How Screening Works

From patient registration to clinical decision — a complete, safe pipeline

01

Patient Registration

Patient registers with basic information, selects preferred language, and provides informed consent for AI-assisted screening.

Consent Language Selection Privacy
02

Retinal Image Capture

Retinal fundus image is captured using compatible camera or uploaded. System validates format, size, and basic integrity.

Upload/Capture Format Check Size Validation
03

Image Quality Gate

Automated checks for blur, brightness, field-of-view, artifacts. Poor quality → recapture requested. No bad image reaches AI.

Critical Safety Step Blur Check Brightness Artifacts
04

Preprocessing

Image undergoes normalization, denoising, and CLAHE contrast enhancement to standardize input for the AI model.

Normalization Denoising CLAHE
05

AI Screening

Deep learning model classifies the image into DR grades. Confidence score displayed only if the model genuinely provides it.

5-Grade Classification Confidence Model Versioning
06

Grad-CAM Explanation

Heatmap overlay shows which regions influenced the AI. Zoom, pan, adjustable opacity. Patient-friendly + clinician explanations.

Heatmap Overlay ROI Explanation
07

Professional Review

A qualified healthcare professional reviews every AI result. AI assists screening — the clinical decision is always human.

Human-in-the-Loop Clinical Decision
08

Referral & Follow-up

If needed, patient is referred to nearest verified eye-care provider. Follow-up scheduling and tracking included.

Referral Nearby Eye Care Follow-up

DR Classification Grades

No Apparent DR No visible signs of diabetic retinopathy
Mild NPDR Microaneurysms only
Moderate NPDR More than microaneurysms but less than severe
Severe NPDR Extensive hemorrhages, venous beading
Proliferative DR Neovascularization, vitreous hemorrhage
Ungradable Image quality insufficient for grading

AI screening is an assistive tool. All results require professional clinical review before any medical decision.

Accessibility

Built for Rural India

Designed to work where it matters most — primary health centres, screening camps, and remote villages

Low Bandwidth Mode

Image compression, optimized API calls, minimal data transfer. Works on 2G/3G networks.

Sync Architecture

Queue-based uploads, failed-upload retry, resume interrupted workflows. No data loss.

Mobile-First UI

Designed for smartphones and tablets. Simple interface for low-literacy users with voice guidance.

Healthcare Worker Mode

Trained ASHA/ANM workers can conduct screening with guided workflows. No specialist required on-site.

Screening Camp Support

Mobile screening camp workflow architecture. Register → Screen → Results → Refer in a single session.

Tele-Ophthalmology

Remote specialist review architecture. Rural screening → City specialist review → Patient gets result.

Honesty

Transparency & Limitations

We believe responsible AI means being honest about what we can and cannot do

What NetraDR Does

  • AI-assisted screening for Diabetic Retinopathy
  • Explainable AI with Grad-CAM visualizations
  • Image quality validation before screening
  • Multilingual, accessible interface
  • Human-in-the-loop clinical review
  • Rural-optimized low-bandwidth architecture

Current Limitations

  • Clinical validation — not yet completed
  • Offline mode — to be validated
  • Not all languages fully translated/tested
  • AI model performance — to be benchmarked on Indian populations
  • Regulatory approval — not obtained
  • This is a prototype, not a certified medical device
